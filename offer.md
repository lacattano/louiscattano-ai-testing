# Offer — AI Test Automation Setup (fixed scope, fixed price)

A defined-scope engagement for teams that want a working Playwright test
suite — built with AI-assisted development — without hiring a permanent
engineer or waiting months for internal tooling.

## What you get

- **A working Playwright pytest suite** covering your agreed critical paths
  (login, checkout, key journeys — you pick the scope)
- **CI integration** (GitHub Actions or your existing CI) — suite runs on
  every push, with a clear pass/fail signal
- **Documentation** — how to run it, how to add tests, how to fix a failure
- **A half-day training session** — your team can take it from here
- **Everything reviewed and shipped by a QA engineer with 6 years'
  experience** — the code is written by LLM agents, but nothing ships without
  my review, my tests, and a green run

## Delivery

- **2 weeks** (part-time or full-time, whichever fits)
- Weekly demo so you can see progress and redirect
- Delivery ends with a passing suite against your site, not a hand-wave

## Price

- **£2,000–£4,000** depending on scope (number of journeys, auth complexity,
  CI setup)
- Fixed price agreed up front; no surprises

## What's excluded (so we both know the edges)

- Performance/load testing
- Mobile or desktop-app automation
- Ongoing maintenance (available separately as a retainer or contract)

## How it works

1. **Discovery call** — you tell me the critical paths; I confirm scope + price
2. **Build** — I design the spec, orchestrate the build, review everything
3. **Verify** — suite runs green against your site, CI wired up
4. **Handover** — docs + half-day training, then it's yours

## Why AI-assisted?

Because the same scope delivered the traditional way takes 4–6 weeks and more
money. AI-assisted delivery compresses the build — and the QA discipline on
top is what makes that speed safe. This is the workflow, not a gimmick: it's
how I built [TanCat](https://github.com/tancat-ai/tancat)
solo — open source (Apache-2.0), 3,100+ tests, a 97.9% static-eval accuracy
gate, CI green on every commit.
